🇸🇬 CoParenting SG — 6 Logo Concepts

Handcrafted vector SVG logos designed specifically for coparentingsg.org

Concept 1 National Flower

The Singapore Vanda Bloom

Two protective teal petals sheltering a vibrant red orchid bud in the center (national flower symbol).

Concept 2 Garden City

The Garden City Tree of Life

Inspired by Singapore's Supertree and Rain Tree canopy—two parent branches uniting to shelter the child.

Concept 3 National Emblem

The Crescent & Guiding Stars

Re-interprets Singapore's crescent and five stars as dual embracing parent arcs guiding the child star.

Concept 4 Landmark

The Singapore River Bridge

Inspired by Jubilee and Helix bridges—two spans connecting seamlessly to carry the child across water.

Concept 5 Heartlands

The Heartland Courtyard

Two residential blocks (mom's home & dad's home) linked by a central playground where the child plays happily.

Concept 6 App / Digital

The Singapore App Monogram

Modern LifeSG/Singpass digital style: rounded badge with interlocking "C", central child dot, and Singapore star.
