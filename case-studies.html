Core Principles Shared by Singaporean and Expatriate Co-Parents

Across both local MOE schooling and international expat contexts, parents who achieve stability focus on these shared foundations:

1. Unambiguous Specificity

Whether calculating MOE tuition caps or international school currency conversions, replace "reasonable agreement" with fixed dates, caps, and timelines.

2. Legal Enforceability

Consent Orders in the Singapore Family Justice Courts and Mirror Orders in overseas jurisdictions protect both parents against cross-border jurisdictional uncertainty.

3. Child-Centred Routines

From bedtime routines across timezones to morning school commutes, the physical energy and emotional security of the child always guide decisions.
